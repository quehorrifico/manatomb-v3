# Versioned CPU defaults

`../default-catalog.json` records the exact source path/hash and packaged deck hash, pin and play style. All five lists match the pinned upstream Commander precons after removing edition and foil decoration. No cards were removed to bypass prompts or AI notices.

Each default reached a natural CPU win through the actual browser; Feline also supplied the human deck in the realistic turn-9 interrupted run. Full human-seat coverage of every list is not claimed. See `docs/v2/evidence/V2-M2.md`. CPU pregame notices flag selected cards as less well supported by the AI; these defaults do not imply tuned difficulty or flawless tactics. The setup inspector returns the exact packaged list.

- **Arcane Wizardry**: Wizards, enter-the-battlefield effects and graveyard value.
- **Draconic Domination**: Five-color ramp into large Dragons.
- **Feline Ferocity**: Cats and equipment; creature combat.
- **Open Hostility**: Four-color creature pressure and attack rewards.
- **Vampiric Bloodlust**: Vampires, token production and sacrifice value.
